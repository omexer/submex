== Description ==

**Submex is a WooCommerce Subscription plugin that helps you sell products and services on a recurring basis, collect payments automatically, and keep subscribers longer.**

Whether you ship a monthly box, run a membership site, sell online courses, or split a high-ticket purchase into installments, Submex gives you one place to create subscription plans, bill customers on schedule, handle failed payments, and see how your recurring revenue is performing.

If you can create a regular WooCommerce product, you can create a subscription with Submex. There is no separate product architecture to learn, and a guided setup wizard gets your first plan live in minutes.

= Why store owners choose Submex =

* **Fast to launch.** Turn simple or variable products into subscriptions with a few clicks and a multi-step setup wizard.
* **Hands-off billing.** Renewals are charged automatically, failed payments are retried, and customers receive the right emails at the right time.
* **Built to retain.** Pause, skip, reschedule, grace periods, cancellation surveys, and win-back campaigns help you keep revenue you would otherwise lose.
* **Fewer support tickets.** Customers manage their own plans from My Account, so routine changes never reach your inbox.
* **Works with your stack.** Connect 14 payment gateways, LMS plugins, CRMs, automation tools, license managers, and community platforms.
* **Clear numbers.** Track MRR, churn, and revenue at risk without exporting a single spreadsheet.

= Subscription products and billing =

**Subscriptions on any product.** Add recurring billing to simple products and variable products, physical or digital. No special product type is required.

**Flexible billing cycles.** Charge daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly. Set a fixed term for plans that should end, or leave them open-ended for evergreen billing.

**Free trials.** Let shoppers try before they pay. You control the trial length and when the first charge happens, which lowers the barrier to signing up.

**Sign-up fees.** Add a one-time setup or onboarding fee on top of the recurring price, set per product.

**Custom renewal pricing.** Charge a different price on renewal than on the first payment, so you can reward loyal subscribers or adjust pricing as your product grows.

**Multiple quantities.** Let customers subscribe to more than one unit of the same product.

**One-time purchase option.** Offer the same product as a subscription or as a single purchase, side by side.

**Automatic billing with manual fallback.** Renewals are charged automatically through your connected gateway. For customers who prefer control, manual renewal sends an invoice they can pay on demand, and early renewal lets them renew before the due date.

= Subscription boxes =

Build-a-box and curated box products let customers choose what goes into each delivery. You set the box size, the products to choose from, and the box discount, and the price follows the contents.

Shoppers can add items from any product page or from "Frequently bought together," buy a box once or on subscription, and edit their next box from My Account until it locks. You plan editions ahead and pack from the pick list and shipments screens.

= Installment payments and split payments =

Not every product suits an ongoing subscription. With installment plans, customers pay for a one-off purchase in a fixed number of charges on a schedule you define (weekly, monthly, or a custom interval). Billing stops automatically after the final payment. You can collect an upfront down payment and allow customers to pay off the remaining balance early.

It is a practical way to sell higher-priced items and reduce cart abandonment without adding a third-party financing service.

= Failed payment recovery =

Failed renewals are a leading cause of involuntary churn. Submex helps you win those payments back:

* **Smart retry logic** with customizable retry intervals
* **Grace period** so customers have time to fix payment details before the subscription is cancelled
* **Card expiry reminders** and failed payment alerts
* **Secure card update links** so customers can update a payment method without logging in
* **Last-retry urgency emails** and renewal reminders

= Retention and win-back tools =

* **Pause, skip, reschedule, and restart** options for subscribers
* **Scheduled cancellation** so customers cancel at the end of the billing period and you still collect for the current cycle
* **Smart cancellation flows** that remind customers of their benefits, collect feedback, and present retention offers before they leave
* **Cancellation survey** to learn why subscribers leave
* **Reactivate on-hold subscriptions** from the admin in a few steps

= Upgrades, downgrades, and proration =

Let customers move between plans without contacting you. Submex calculates proration automatically for mid-cycle changes. Create upgrade groups with tier hierarchies, upgrade incentives, display conditions, and upgrade prompts, then track how they perform.

= Recurring coupons and access control =

* **Recurring coupons:** apply percentage or fixed-amount discounts to the first payment only, or carry them through a set number of renewals. Set usage limits and expiry dates.
* **Role-based product visibility:** show or hide subscription products by user role to create member-only offers.
* **Membership plans and access rules:** create plans, grant or cancel memberships, and restrict posts, pages, and other content to active members.
* **Digital file access control:** deliver downloads only to customers with an active subscription, and revoke access automatically when payments fail.

= Subscription management for admins =

Manage every subscription from one dashboard. See who is active, who is about to renew, and who is at risk, then change a billing cycle, switch a plan, or cancel on a customer's behalf without leaving WordPress.

* Complete subscription table with status, next payment date, expiry date, and billing frequency
* Quick customer search and real-time status tracking
* Manual subscription orders: create and assign subscriptions to customers with your own intervals, expiry dates, and products
* Full activity timeline and logs for every subscription, with filtering
* Error logs for troubleshooting subscription issues
* Renewal order history
* Delivery schedules, delivery status management, and printable delivery addresses for physical products
* A shortcode to display a subscription table anywhere on your site

= Customer self-service =

Subscribers get a dashboard inside My Account where they can see upcoming payments and amounts, review payment history, update addresses and payment methods, renew early, pay off installments, change plans, pause or skip subscriptions, stop automatic renewals, and cancel their subscriptions. Giving customers greater control and flexibility means fewer emails and support requests for you to answer.

== Installation ==

= Automatic installation =

1. In your WordPress dashboard, go to **Plugins → Add New**.
2. Search for **Submex**.
3. Click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.
4. Make sure WooCommerce is installed and active.
5. Follow the setup wizard to configure your first subscription plan and connect a payment gateway.

= Manual installation =

1. Download the plugin zip file.
2. Go to **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin**, choose the file, and click **Install Now**.
3. Activate the plugin and complete the setup wizard.

= Creating your first subscription =

1. Go to **Products → Add New** (or edit an existing product).
2. Enable the subscription option and choose the billing interval.
3. Add a free trial, sign-up fee, or expiry if you want them.
4. Publish the product, then place a test order to confirm everything works.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= What is Submex? =

Submex is a WooCommerce Subscription plugin that lets you sell products and services on a recurring basis. It handles automatic billing, renewals, free trials, installments, customer self-service, and subscription analytics.

= Does Submex require WooCommerce? =

Yes. Submex is an extension for WooCommerce and requires WooCommerce to be installed and active.

= Can I turn any WooCommerce product into a subscription? =

Yes. You can add subscription billing to simple and variable products, including physical and downloadable items.

= Which billing intervals are supported? =

Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly. You can also set an expiry or fixed term, or leave a subscription open-ended.

= Which payment gateways work with Submex? =

Stripe, PayPal, Paddle, Mollie, Razorpay, Xendit, Square, Authorize.Net, Braintree, Adyen, GoCardless, WooPayments, bKash, and SSLCommerz.

= Are renewals charged automatically? =

Yes. Renewals are charged automatically through your connected gateway. You can also enable manual renewal, where customers receive an invoice and pay on demand.

= What happens when a payment fails? =

Submex retries the payment on the schedule you set, sends failed payment and card expiry emails, and provides secure links for customers to update their card. A grace period gives customers extra time before the subscription is cancelled.

= Can customers manage their own subscriptions? =

Yes. From My Account, customers can view billing dates, update their payment method and shipping address, renew early, pause or skip, change plans, and cancel.

= Can I offer free trials and sign-up fees? =

Yes. Both can be configured per product, and they can be combined.

= Can I sell installment plans instead of subscriptions? =

Yes. Installment plans split a one-time purchase into a fixed number of payments on your schedule, and billing stops after the last payment. You can also collect a down payment and allow early payoff.

= Can I sell subscription boxes? =

Yes. You can create build-a-box and curated box products, set pricing based on contents, let customers edit upcoming boxes until they lock, and manage packing with pick lists and shipments.

= Can I restrict content to subscribers or members? =

Yes. You can create membership plans, restrict posts, pages, and other content to active members, and limit digital file downloads to active subscribers.

= Does Submex work with LMS plugins? =

Yes. Submex integrates with Tutor LMS, Academy LMS, LearnPress, and LearnDash so you can sell course access on a recurring basis.

= Does Submex integrate with CRMs and automation tools? =

Yes. It works with MailPoet, FluentCRM, WP Fusion, OttoKit, Bit Integrations, and AutomatorWP.

= Can I see my MRR and churn rate? =

Yes. The analytics dashboard shows MRR, ARR, churn, subscriber growth, and revenue at risk.

= Does Submex have a REST API? =

Yes. The REST API lets you retrieve subscription details for specific users, and the plugin provides action hooks and filters for custom development.

= Is Submex compatible with multilingual sites? =

Yes. Submex supports WPML and Polylang.

= Is Submex compatible with WooCommerce Blocks and the block editor? =

Yes. Submex supports WooCommerce Blocks and Gutenberg.

= Is there a limit on subscriptions or subscribers? =

No. You can create unlimited subscription products and accept unlimited subscribers.

Visit the support forum on WordPress.org or the plugin documentation. Replace this answer with your preferred support and documentation links before publishing.
